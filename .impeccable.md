## Design Context

### Users
Locals and visitors around Kansas City metro deciding where to eat or drink, mostly on a phone, often at night. Jobs: find the menu, hours and happy hour, order or call, book events and catering.

### Brand Personality
Warm, festive, cantina after dark. Premium Mexican grill: Josper-grilled, tequila-forward, tile and arches. Not corporate, not kitsch.

### Aesthetic Direction
"Salvia y noche": sage ground #9fadae with ink #17323a text, deep teal sections for impact, marigold only on dark, one rose block per page. Talavera tile bands, arches, offset frames, full-bleed video hero. Single display face (Eudora, lowercase) plus Figtree; no script/display mixing in headlines. Leans premium/cantina. Anti-references: generic card grids, side-stripe borders, gradient text.

### Design Principles
1. Light/dark rhythm: never two identical grounds back to back.
2. Ink on sage, ivory/white/marigold on deep. Never marigold on sage.
3. One typeface voice per headline.
4. Photos and tile carry the warmth; cards stay rare.
5. Motion is CSS-only, ease-out, reduced-motion safe.
