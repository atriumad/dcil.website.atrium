# Web page templates

Every page: sticky `NavBar` (cream) → content sections on `cream` separated by `space-8` → `Newsletter` → `Footer`. One loud band per screen height.

## Home (v4)
1. Flat white `NavBar`.
2. `Hero` photo — "Real-deal *Mexican* flavor from our family to yours", View Menu + Find a Location, table photo on the rose band, flowers, postmark, fiesta `TileBand`.
3. `ValueProps`.
4. `ColorSplit` marigold — where it all started (León story) with polaroid.
5. `Marquee` rose.
6. Explore the menu — `CategoryGrid` (rose, teal, marigold, navy).
7. `ColorSplit` rose, reversed — fresh out of the kitchen.
8. This week — 3 `PosterCard`s (teal, rose, marigold).
9. `SpecialsBoard` on `teal-surface` with the tile band on top.
10. Signature dishes — 3 `DishCard`s.
11. `PromoBanner` marigold — everyday drinks.
12. Locations — 4 `LocationCard`s.
13. `SocialGrid`, `Newsletter` (teal-700 band), `Footer` (rose with fiesta tiles).

## Menu
1. Short page header: `SectionHeader` size l on `cream` with doodles `Pattern` behind.
2. Sticky `CategoryTabs` under the nav.
3. `MenuSection`s in two columns; one `featured` item per section; `TileBand` agave between food and drinks.
4. `PromoBanner` rose for catering or Happy Hour.
5. Small print (`small`): "Prices and availability may vary by location."

## Specials / Happy Hour
1. `Hero` poster — hand-lettered title with the `!` swap (DA!LY SPEC!ALS, HAPPY HOUR).
2. `SpecialsBoard` full width.
3. `PromoBanner` with the drink deals and hours.

## Locations (index + detail)
- Index: `SectionHeader` + `LocationCard` grid + map embed in a `radius-xl` frame with 3px `ink` border.
- Detail: `Hero` split with the location photo, hours table (LocationCard data), `DishCard`s of local favorites, directions `Button` (icon `arrow-up-right`).

## About / Nuestra historia
`Hero` split with a family/table photo → two `FeatureSplit`s alternating `reverse` → `Marquee` agave → `h2`/`body-lg` long-form in `container-narrow`.

## Catering / Events
`FeatureSplit` + form with `Input`s (name, email, phone, date, guests) + `Button` primary "Request a Quote".
